In the paper "Analyzing and Improving the Training Dynamics of Diffusion Models" (also known as the EDM2 paper), "EDM2" stands for "Elucidating Diffusion Models 2."
It is the direct sequel to the authors' earlier foundational paper, "Elucidating the Design Space of Diffusion-Based Generative Models" (NeurIPS 2022), commonly referred to as the EDM paper.
